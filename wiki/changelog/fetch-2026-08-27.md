One backlog item resolved to a confirmed DOI: "Maternal influences on infant gut microbiome and health" (Nature, doi:10.1038/s41586-026-10922-9) — metagenomic sequencing of 4,526 longitudinal faecal samples from 714 mother–infant pairs in the Dutch Lifelines NEXT cohort (12 weeks gestation to 1 year postpartum), integrated with 474 clinical/exposure variables and ultra-deep breast-milk/vaginal-microbiome sequencing; finds the maternal gut microbiome is the dominant reservoir for infant gut strains (vaginal/breast-milk transmission only occasional, strain sharing time-dependent and abundance-dependent) and that early strain transmission patterns predict later health outcomes including eczema. Publication date confirmed as 12 August 2026 (previously logged with an approximate 2026-08-13 date and no DOI). Given the shared cohort, mother-infant strain-transmission design, and eczema outcome, this is very likely the same study as the separately-tracked "The maternal gut microbiome is a predictor of infant eczema" lead (attributed to Antman/Lewin-Epstein/Zeevi, purportedly 2026-08-14) rather than a distinct paper — flagging for human confirmation to merge or discard the duplicate entry rather than resolving unilaterally.
