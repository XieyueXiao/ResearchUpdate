## Papers Found (2026-09-09)

### 1. Integrating multi-omics technologies to decipher microbiome functions
- **Journal:** Nature Communications (IF ~17)
- **Highlights:** Perspective by Eriksen, Chatzigiannidou & Brix synthesizing the state of multi-omics microbiome research (metagenomics, metatranscriptomics, metaproteomics, metabolomics) and the barriers to combining these layers into unified functional models. The authors outline key obstacles in data integration and metadata harmonization across studies and cohorts, and propose a roadmap toward scalable, FAIR-compliant multi-omics investigations, including where AI/ML methods could help resolve cross-omics linkage and causal inference. Positioned as a field-level agenda-setting piece rather than new primary data.
- **Link:** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-77539-4)
