### Off-window / off-list notes
- **Microbe-derived NAD⁺ precursor drives gut stem-cell renewal (Rutgers; *Science Advances*, not a target journal; paper dated 2026-11-06, press 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-23):** "The (amide-)N-methylnicotinamide is a microbe-derived NAD⁺ precursor that drives gut stem cell renewal". Bacterial N-MNA, unlike host-made MNA, can be salvaged back into NAD⁺. It supplied about 22% of NAD in human colon cells within 24 h. It sped epithelial repair in DSS-colitis mice (about 5% versus 20% weight loss, with untreated mice dying) and raised 7-day survival in injured flies from 43% to 78%. IBD stool was profiled, but N-MNA was not tested in patients. Links: [EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1151827), [News-Medical](https://news-medical.net/news/20261113/Researchers-discover-bacterial-molecule-that-rapidly-repairs-intestinal-damage.aspx)
- **Peripheral CB1 antagonist AM6545 in a fragile X mouse model (Pompeu Fabra; *Translational Psychiatry*, not a target journal; about 2026-11-19, date seen in only one source):** a one-week treatment improved hippocampal synaptic connectivity and memory. It also restored reduced *Lactobacillus* and *Akkermansia* in the mice, which points to the gut–brain axis. Link: [EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1152509)
- **Slfn4 in macrophages and colitis/CAC (*Communications Medicine*, not a target journal; 2026-11-17):** Slfn4-deficient mice had worse DSS colitis and colitis-associated cancer, with dysbiosis. FMT and co-housing suggest the microbiota contributes. Link: [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43856-026-02012-8)
- **pks⁺ *E. coli* persistence in CRC (*Cancer Causes & Control*, not a target journal; 2026-11-11):** metagenomes from 638 people showed pks⁺ *E. coli* in 34.7% of CRC patients versus 17.2% of controls (OR 2.76). Link: [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10552-026-02280-8)
- **Nature Microbiology research briefing (2026-11-20):** ultra-deep metagenomics of the manatee gut (non-human, so out of scope). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41564-026-02529-7)
