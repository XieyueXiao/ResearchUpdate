### 1. From molecules to medicine: the gut‒heart axis comes of age
- **Journal:** Gut Microbes (IF ~12)
- **Highlights:** A review/perspective tracing the maturation of gut–heart axis research from correlative microbiome-cardiovascular associations toward mechanistic and translational understanding. Synthesizes evidence on microbiota-derived metabolites (e.g., TMAO, SCFAs, secondary bile acids) and bacterial structural components acting on cardiac and vascular tissue, and discusses the path from observational cohort associations to causal, mechanism-based and therapeutically actionable models of host–microbe cardiovascular crosstalk.
