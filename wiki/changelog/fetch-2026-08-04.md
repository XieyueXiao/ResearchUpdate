## Papers Found (2026-08-04)

### 1. Systematic evaluation of PASEF acquisition strategies in complex metaproteomes
- **Journal:** Nature Communications (IF ~17)
- **Highlights:** University of Vienna researchers (Feng Xian et al., Bruker Daltonics Center of Excellence for Metaproteomics with the Systems Biology of Pain Laboratory) systematically benchmark five state-of-the-art PASEF mass-spectrometry acquisition strategies (DDA-, DIA-, Slice-, Synchro-, and midia-PASEF) for gut metaproteomics using human fecal samples. The best-performing approaches deliver high sensitivity, reproducibility, and functional resolution, capturing concordant host and microbial protein-level responses through the onset and recovery of intestinal inflammation — coordinated shifts in microbial functional pathways alongside the host epithelial repair response. The work provides a practical methodological roadmap for making gut metaproteomics more sensitive, reproducible, and accessible for future host–microbiome studies.
- **Link:** [doi:10.1038/s41467-026-75845-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-75845-5)
