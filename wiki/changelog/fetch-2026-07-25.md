## Papers Found (2026-07-25)

### 1. Microbial competition, host defences and Salmonella Typhimurium countermeasures determine intestinal infection outcome (review)
- **Journal:** Nature Reviews Microbiology (IF ~69)
- **Highlights:** Review by Vázquez-Torres synthesizing how the dynamic interplay between commensal microbial competition (colonization resistance), host innate/adaptive defences, and Salmonella enterica serovar Typhimurium's own countermeasures jointly determines whether intestinal infection is established or cleared. Covers mechanisms by which the gut microbiota restricts nutrient/niche access and produces inhibitory metabolites, how host epithelial and immune responses shape the luminal environment during infection, and the counter-strategies Salmonella deploys (e.g., exploiting inflammation-driven dysbiosis) to overcome colonization resistance. Directly relevant to the knowledge base's colonization-resistance and host–microbe interaction scope. Published online 2026-07-23.
