## Papers Found (2026-11-14)

### 1. Effect of two common GVHD prophylaxis regimens on the gut microbiome in a randomized phase 3 trial
- **Journal:** Nature Medicine (IF ~82)
- **Highlights:** Graft-versus-host disease (GVHD) prophylaxis after allogeneic hematopoietic cell transplantation (HCT) can change the gut microbiome, which is linked to transplant outcomes, but no randomized comparison of regimens existed. This study is the microbiome companion (BMT CTN 1801) to the phase 3 BMT CTN 1703 trial. It profiled serial stool samples from patients randomized to post-transplant cyclophosphamide/tacrolimus/MMF (PTCy) or to tacrolimus/methotrexate (Tac/MTX). On days 14–28 after HCT, the PTCy arm had lower microbiome diversity and lower absolute microbial load. According to the linked preprint, the PTCy arm also had fewer *Clostridium scindens* and secondary bile acid metabolism pathways, and those pathways were associated with lower chronic GVHD risk. Diversity at engraftment did not predict non-relapse mortality. The authors conclude that the microbiome's role in post-transplant immune modulation depends on the prophylaxis regimen. *Caveat:* WebSearch dated this paper 2026-11-12, just before the 24-hour window; it is included because the 2026-11-13 fetch did not capture it. The mechanistic details come from the preprint version and should be checked against the published paper.
- **Link:** [Nature Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04712-z) (DOI presumed 10.1038/s41591-026-04712-z)
