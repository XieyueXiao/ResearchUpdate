- **Frontiers in Nutrition (not a target journal), 2026-11-23:** a multi-omics study of children with functional dyspepsia. It asks whether gut microbiota mediate the link between diet (for example, fibre intake) and gastric symptoms, and reports associations between specific bacterial genera and dietary intake. Link: [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2026.2008867/full)
- **Fibre deprivation and mucus foraging (Princeton/Ludwig; coverage dated 2026-11-09 to 2026-11-17):** a mouse study found that fibre-starved gut bacteria degrade colonic mucus and produce potentially harmful compounds. A companion study reports that digestion-resistant plant proteins ("Prif") shift bacterial metabolism toward beneficial products. Sources disagree on the journal: one names Cell and PNAS, ScienceDaily names Nature Metabolism, and IBTimes dates the mouse work to July. Verify before ingesting. Possibly related to the "recycled 2016 Cell fibre/mucus" coverage noted in the 2026-11-19 fetch.

The journal is not stated in the coverage. Results are preliminary.
